Meet the Founder

Built by someone
who gets it.

I'm Cinth — founder of Cinfluence and the person you'll actually work with. This studio exists because I saw a gap: beauty, wellness, and aesthetic brands deserve a content partner who understands their world, speaks their audience's language, and treats their brand with the care it's earned.

My background spans social media management, influencer and UGC campaign management, AI-powered content production, e-commerce content, and brand identity work — across beauty, wellness, fashion, lifestyle, and more. I've managed platforms, directed content, sourced creators, and built content systems that run without the founder having to think about them every day.

Cinfluence is deliberately small. I take on a limited number of clients at any one time — not because I can't scale, but because depth is the product. When you work with Cinfluence, you work with me.

What Drives the Work

Studio Values

01

Strategy First

Every post, every Reel, every campaign begins with a clear purpose. I don't produce for the sake of producing — each piece of content earns its place in the calendar before it gets made.

02

Quality Over Volume

Fewer, considered pieces outperform volume every time. I hold the work — and your brand — to a standard that reflects the care you put into your own product or service.

03

Consistency Builds Authority

Brands that show up consistently, with intention, earn trust over time. I build the systems and workflows that make sure your presence never slips — even when you're focused on everything else.

04

Honest Partnership

I'll tell you what I think — including when a direction won't serve you. The goal is always a brand that grows with substance, not content that flatters in the short term and disappears.

The Difference

Why brands choose Cinfluence

Specialist, not generalist

I work specifically in beauty, wellness, and aesthetics. That means I already understand your audience, the visual language of your category, and what makes your clients trust a brand before they book.

I produce, not just manage

I don't outsource or simply post content you supply. I build the strategy, write the scripts, design the graphics, edit the videos, and manage the platforms — all with consistent creative direction.

AI without the compromise

I use AI to scale production intelligently, not to replace brand voice. Every AI-assisted piece goes through editorial review before it reaches your audience.

Founder-to-founder access

You work directly with me — not a junior account manager. The person you speak to in the discovery call is the person who does the work.

Full creative visibility

You review every piece of content before it goes live. Your brand, your approval, your control — with none of the day-to-day execution burden on your plate.

Small roster by design

I keep my client list small so every brand gets the attention it deserves. When you're a Cinfluence client, your work is never deprioritised for someone else's.

Industries I Know
